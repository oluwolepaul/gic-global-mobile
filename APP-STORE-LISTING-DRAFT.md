# GIC Global Connect — App Store listing draft

## Product page copy

**App name:** GIC Global Connect  
**Subtitle:** Worship, grow, stay connected  
**Promotional text:** Find your GIC campus, watch messages, read the Weekly Word, explore ministries, pray, give, and serve.

**Description**

GIC Global Connect is the official app of Grace International Church. Stay connected with your church community wherever you are.

**Find your home campus**
- Explore GIC locations, service times, directions, and campus-specific events.
- Discover ministries for every season of life and connect with a ministry.

**Grow in the Word**
- Watch recent messages from the GIC YouTube channel inside the app.
- Read the Weekly Word, with teaching notes and Scripture references.
- Join live services and worship with the GIC family.

**Take your next step**
- Share a prayer request or contact the church.
- Explore ways to serve and start a worker registration.
- Give through GIC’s official giving page.

Grace International Church is a multicultural, multiethnic family of faith with communities in Houston, New York, and Lagos, and worship online.

Internet access is required for live streams, sermon videos, event updates, and online giving. Some features open the GIC website, YouTube, maps, or your email app.

## App Review notes draft

No account is required. The app provides campus details and events, ministry information, weekly teaching notes, live and recorded YouTube content, and contact/prayer/worker forms. Forms open the user’s email app; the user reviews and sends the message. Online giving opens GIC’s giving page in the browser. Internet access is required for streamed and web content.
