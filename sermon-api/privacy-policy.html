Privacy Policy

For GIC Global Connect · Last updated November 7, 2026

Grace Covenant Ministries, Inc. (“GIC,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the GIC Global Connect mobile app.

Information you choose to send

The app includes optional contact, ministry connection, worker registration, and prayer request forms. You may enter information such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred campus, ministry interest, and message. When you choose to continue, the app prepares an email in your email app. The message is not sent to GIC until you review it and tap Send. If you cancel or do not send it, GIC does not receive it through that form. The app does not save these form entries on the device or send them to a GIC app server.

After GIC receives your email, authorized church staff may use the information to respond to your message, prayer request, or interest in serving or a ministry. Messages are handled in the church’s email and records systems. You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting info@gicglobal.org; legal, safety, and record-keeping requirements may affect what can be deleted.

Please share only information you are comfortable sending by email. Prayer requests can contain sensitive personal information.

Technical information and service providers

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. To display the public GIC sermon feed, the app contacts a feed service hosted by Vercel. Vercel may process standard request and security information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and protect that service.

Sermon videos are played using YouTube. Google and YouTube may process device, network, and viewing information when you load or watch a video, under their own privacy terms. The app may also open map services, your email app, or other external services when you choose those features. Their privacy policies apply to your use of those services.

Giving

The Give feature opens GIC’s official giving page in your web browser. Payment information is entered with the giving method you choose and is handled by that payment provider under its privacy policy. GIC may receive donation information needed to process, acknowledge, and administer your gift. The app does not collect or store your payment card or account credentials.

How information is shared

GIC does not sell personal information. Information you send to the church is made available to authorized staff and service providers who need it to respond or operate the church’s communication systems. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect people, rights, or safety.

Storage, security, and international processing

Messages sent to GIC are kept in church email and records systems according to the church’s operational and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, though no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. GIC and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States.

Children

The app provides general church information and is not designed to create accounts or collect information directly from children. If a child’s information is sent to GIC and you would like it reviewed or removed, contact us at info@gicglobal.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our information practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact GIC

For questions or privacy requests, email info@gicglobal.org or write to Grace Covenant Ministries, Inc., 15401 Bellaire Blvd, Houston, TX 77083, United States.
